* **No Lawyer-Client Relationship:** Using this platform, its features, or contacting us does not create a lawyer-client relationship.
* **No Legal Interpretation:** The App does not interpret laws, legal tests, or predict legal outcomes.
* **Accuracy of Information:** All information provided by the App or its AI features may be incomplete or inaccurate. All decisions must be made by the user, not the AI.
* **Required Review:** All AI-generated outputs, chronologies, summaries, and suggestions must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before use in any legal proceeding, dispute, or transaction.

## 3. Privacy & Data Protection (Canada)
The App complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and the British Columbia Personal Information Protection Act (BC PIPA).
* **Data Minimization:** We only collect the personal information necessary to deliver App features.
* **User Rights:** You have the right to access, export, correct, and delete your data at any time.
* **No Selling/Transferring:** We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
* **No AI Training:** We do not train AI models on user data without explicit consent.
* **Encryption Standards:** All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2+.
* **Audit Logs:** We maintain immutable audit logs for all data access, edits, and AI actions.
* **Data Retention:** Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

## 4. Security Requirements
* **Data Protection:** High-security AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit.
* **Role-Based Access Control:** Strict division of privileges between user accounts and lawyer accounts.
* **Audit Trails:** Automatic logging of AI suggestions, data edits, logins, and lawyer access.
* **Secure Authentication:** Secure OAuth flows (Google, Apple, Clio) and password hashing using bcrypt or argon2.

## 5. AI-Specific Rules
* **No Legal Interpretation:** AI features will not interpret laws, provide legal advice, or predict outcomes.
* **No Fact Generation:** The AI only analyzes user-provided information. It will not generate new facts or assumptions.
* **User Confirmation:** Every AI suggestion must be confirmed (Accepted / Declined) by the user.
* **AI Disclaimer:** Every suggestion displays the notice: *“I am a tool, not a lawyer. This is not legal advice.”*
* **Citations:** Suggestions will always cite the user's own timeline entries.
* **No Alteration:** The AI only annotates timeline records; it will never modify user data.
* **Consent Gate:** AI analysis requires explicit user consent before processing.
